What we build

Not single buildings. Whole neighbourhoods, one after another, along the same road.

  • Apartments

    2 BHK and 3 BHK homes in mid-rise and high-rise towers — the core of every Atharva Nagari from I to VIII.

  • Row houses

    Independent 4 BHK row houses alongside the apartment towers at Atharva Nagari III.

  • Shops at the gate

    Ground-floor commercial units at Atharva Nagari V, VI and VII, so daily needs sit within the township.

  • Shared ground

    Gardens, children's play areas, gymnasiums and club houses, planned project by project.
