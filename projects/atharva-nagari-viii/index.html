The project

Overview

The eighth Atharva township, on four acres along the road where the first seven were built.

Atharva Nagari VIII is planned as 2 BHK and 3 BHK apartments, with a club house, park, gymnasium and children's play area within the compound. It sits on Besa–Pipla Road near St. Vincent Pallotti School, a short drive from the completed Atharva Nagari townships you can visit today.
